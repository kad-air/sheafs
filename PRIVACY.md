# Sheafs privacy policy

Sheafs collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it
does not send anything to its developer.

## What stays on your device

Your books, their covers, your reading positions, highlights and notes, and your settings are stored
on your device, inside the app. The Home Screen widget reads the book you opened last from the same
place. Deleting Sheafs deletes all of it.

## What leaves your device, and only if you set it up

- **OPDS catalogs.** When you add a catalog, Sheafs connects to the address you entered to browse it
  and download books. A catalog's username and password are kept in the iOS keychain and sent only
  to that catalog.
- **KOReader sync.** If you sign in to a sync server (kosync), Sheafs sends it your username and
  password to sign in (the password is kept in the iOS keychain) and, for each book you read: an
  identifier computed from the book file, your position in the book, how far through it you are, the
  device name you chose, and a random identifier Sheafs created for this installation (not a hardware
  identifier). It sends these only to the server you entered, which you or the server's operator run.

Sheafs talks to no other servers. Those services' own privacy practices apply to what they receive.
